we were learning about poetry in english class today so here is mine =)


the poem's theme is "disappointment", but I think my is overly-exaggerated LOL

(note: the "/" is a paragraph break thingy because I don't know how to do it D=)

________________________________________________________________



My heart sank like 

a stone into murky waters. 

Watching as the dreams, wishes left, 

Dissipating into the shadows. 

Forgotten. 

Abandoned. 

/

Standing there,  

like a clown in spotlight. 

Palms still warm with hope, 

Now warm with shame. 

“Why did I believe them?” 

/

Standing there, 

Perhaps, pinned by the weight of 

promises unkept  

and stories untold, 

and sanctuary unfound. 

Heart aching, from the ruins of longing. 

/

The world held its breath, 

But not for me. 

Thoughts spiralling, freefalling. 

I could help but watch, 

As all the waiting, all the expectation, 

Faded away into nothing.
